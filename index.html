Background

My interdisciplinary foundation spans athletics (table tennis, gymnastics, sailing), Sea Cadet Corps, and early robotics work. From building auto-movable shopping trolleys in middle school to leading teams in national hackathons — each experience has built resilience and problem-solving skills that inform my current research and development work.

I started with robotics kits in 2016, learning circuits and then drive systems (skid-steer, differential drive, three-axis) through EduPrime's PRIME courses, and went on to present projects at StoryOfMakers (2017), Expiscor at the Nehru Science Centre (2017), and INSEF Pune, where a multipurpose farm equipment robot won third prize.
